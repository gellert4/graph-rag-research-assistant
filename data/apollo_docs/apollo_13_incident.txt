Apollo 13 is remembered for an oxygen tank explosion that occurred during the mission. The accident happened after launch and forced the crew to abort the planned lunar landing in the Fra Mauro region. The mission instead relied on the Lunar Module as a lifeboat and was brought back safely to Earth without landing on the Moon.

The incident was significant because it demonstrated the dangers of deep-space missions and led to engineering changes in subsequent Apollo operations. The mission was not a successful lunar landing, but it was a successful rescue and return profile.

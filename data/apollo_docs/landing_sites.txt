The lunar landing sites of Apollo missions were chosen to support science objectives and mission safety. Apollo 11 touched down in the Sea of Tranquility, Apollo 12 landed in the Ocean of Storms, Apollo 14 landed in Fra Mauro, Apollo 15 landed near the Hadley-Apennine region, Apollo 16 landed in the Descartes Highlands, and Apollo 17 landed at Taurus-Littrow. Apollo 13 did not land because of an oxygen tank problem after launch.

These sites were selected for geological diversity, terrain suitability, and operational constraints. Landing site choice had a direct impact on the type of samples and experiments that could be carried out on the lunar surface.

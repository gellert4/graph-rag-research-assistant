Saturn V was the heavy launch vehicle used to send Apollo crews toward the Moon. It was developed specifically to support the Apollo program and was one of the most powerful rockets ever built. Launching the lunar missions required a large payload capability and a staged design that allowed the vehicle to escape Earth and carry the spacecraft toward translunar injection.

The rocket was central to the Apollo mission architecture and made crewed lunar travel possible. Its reliability and power were crucial to the safety and success of the Apollo journey.

The Apollo program represented a major push by NASA to land humans on the Moon and return them safely to Earth. Between 1969 and 1972, a series of missions explored the lunar surface, performed geology experiments, and deployed scientific instruments. Apollo 11 achieved the first crewed landing, while Apollo 17 was the final Apollo mission to land on the Moon.

The program included both landing and orbit-only missions. A number of missions were designed to test lunar landing procedures, geology, and systems reliability. Several missions ended with successful landings, while Apollo 13 was diverted after a serious oxygen system problem.

The Apollo program was a NASA-led effort to land humans on the Moon and return them safely to Earth. It began in the early 1960s and culminated with the Apollo 17 mission in 1972. The program included a combination of crewed lunar landings, orbital missions, and engineering tests meant to extend human knowledge of the lunar surface and planetary science.

NASA used Apollo missions to test lunar landing procedures, geological sampling, and surface exploration. Missions varied in purpose, but the unifying theme was the learning and demonstration of human lunar exploration.

Apollo 11 was the first crewed mission to land on the Moon. On July 20, 1969, Neil Armstrong and Buzz Aldrin descended to the lunar surface while Michael Collins remained in lunar orbit. The mission landed in the Sea of Tranquility.

The primary objective was to perform a lunar landing and return safely to Earth. Armstrong became the first person to walk on the Moon, and the astronauts collected samples, deployed scientific instruments, and returned with film and rocks.

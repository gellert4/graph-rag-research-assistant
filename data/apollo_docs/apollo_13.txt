Apollo 13 was intended to land in the Fra Mauro region, but an oxygen tank explosion aborted the lunar landing shortly after launch. Jim Lovell, Jack Swigert, and Fred Haise were aboard the spacecraft. The crew used the Lunar Module as a lifeboat and safely returned to Earth after a dramatic mission.

This mission became famous for the phrase 'Houston, we’ve had a problem.' The landing site was not reached because the mission was diverted to a safe return trajectory.

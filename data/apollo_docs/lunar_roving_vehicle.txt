The Lunar Roving Vehicle was used on Apollo 15 to support greater exploration range. David Scott and James Irwin used the rover to travel across the lunar surface, collecting samples and carrying equipment. The rover increased the amount of terrain the astronauts could access and improved the efficiency of the mission's scientific work.

Apollo 15 also carried out geological observations in a region near the Apennine Front. This mission was a major step in lunar field geology and helped establish a more systematic approach to exploring the surface.

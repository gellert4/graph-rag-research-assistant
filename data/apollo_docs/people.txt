Neil Armstrong was the commander of Apollo 11 and the first person to walk on the Moon. Buzz Aldrin was the lunar module pilot of Apollo 11 and walked on the Moon with Armstrong. Michael Collins orbited the Moon in Apollo 11 while Armstrong and Aldrin descended to the surface. Charles Conrad Jr. and Alan Bean landed on Apollo 12. Richard Gordon remained in lunar orbit during Apollo 12. Jim Lovell, Jack Swigert, and Fred Haise flew Apollo 13, but the mission did not land on the Moon. Alan Shepard and Edgar Mitchell landed on Apollo 14. Stuart Roosa stayed in orbit during Apollo 14. David Scott and James Irwin landed on Apollo 15. Alfred Worden remained in orbit during Apollo 15. John Young and Charles Duke landed on Apollo 16. Ken Mattingly remained in lunar orbit during Apollo 16. Eugene Cernan and Harrison Schmitt landed on Apollo 17. Ronald Evans remained in orbit during Apollo 17. 

NASA managed the Apollo program and organized lunar missions. Kennedy Space Center was the launch site for many Apollo missions. The Apollo missions were designed to explore the Moon and advance lunar science.
